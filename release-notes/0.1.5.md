An age you can read, and a countdown to each repository's next read.

- **Age, in days and hours.** The quiet column is now headed "age": one thin bar and a figure such as `4d 07h` or `21h` (`0h` within the hour). The drawer and "needs effort" say the same figure.
- **Each repository counts down to its next read.** Its line ends in the same thin bar, whose white is the time left, and the minutes to go: `-4m`, up to `0m`. The two bars and their key in the top line are gone.
- **A narrow window gives up the age first**, so progress and the other columns stay longer.
- **A move that falls to an organization is yours where your access lets you make it.** A bot's pull request, or one from a fork, that waits on the repository's organization now reads "you @ Acme", with the yellow dot, where your rights there are enough to merge it or to ask for its review.
- **Signing in with another account starts clean.** What the account before had read no longer stays on the board.
- **The window's title shows the version, and a hammer for each repository watched.**
