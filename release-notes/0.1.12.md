The cards at the top keep their room for your agents.

- **Fewer people are named among the cards.** Before, everyone a pull request waited on had a card. On a repository with many contributors most of them had no agents, so each was a bare name, and together they folded your own card down to a few agents. Now someone with no agents is named only when they hold up a pull request you wrote or have an agent on. The others are still in the list, in what needs effort and in the `○n` counts.
- **Names with no agents share one card.** They stand one under another beside the cards, five to a column at the most, in the rows the strip already has.
- **Your agents keep their room.** The names take what the cards leave. When the window is too narrow for them all, the last names give way and a line says how many (`3 more`).
- **The order is you, then people with agents, then the names**, with your teams first among them.
- **`tab` steps through who is shown.** The name in focus is underlined, and keeps its place when the window narrows.
