Your team's agents on the board, keys you can find at a glance, and updates that are checked more strictly.

- **Agents are on the board**, when an [agent-grange](https://github.com/djp3/agent-grange) runs on this Mac. Each sits beneath the person who runs it, on the pull request its name says (`proj-86-pr-98`), with what it is doing: at work, at its prompt, waiting, or unknown. A pull request an agent is at work on comes off your list. There is nothing to set: part 4 of the setup says what the grange reports.
- **The board says when its agents are missing.** While the grange is out of date, or has stopped answering, the end of the ticker's line says so, and the setup shows how to fix it.
- **The keys at the foot of each screen are lit**, with what each does beside it. On the setup they now fit the window: the way back to the board and the way out always stay.
- **Closing the window quits the app**, also while an update is being offered.
- **Updates are checked more strictly.** From this version on the app takes an update only when the list that announces it is signed too, and checks each update before it is unpacked.
- **The app carries the licenses of Sparkle and SwiftTerm**, beside its own.
